## The open decision

She has marked the week unavailable and asked to come off the two shifts already published in it. The requests were filed nine days ahead (nominal T): in time by the seven days the schedule rules ask for a shift, late by the fourteen the handbook asks for a week off; she had asked slot 4 by word of mouth before. She books no customer to herself inside the week. Nothing is decided: her published shifts still stand, the draft days have no advisor at her desk, and the cover requirement is not met there.

`east` sells less recommended work without her, and the average order falls with it (per market, below).

**The past case.** While the diagnostician was away, diagnostic orders at `main` waited 2.5 days from opening to work, against half a day normally; eleven were sent to `east`; the flagship's billed hours in those two weeks were 18 % under the four weeks before. Leave has no record of its own: it is availability rows, no shifts and a personal diary entry.
