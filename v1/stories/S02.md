## Wrinkle

F1 took cash and hid it three ways. Each way is a separate set of rows; the three do not overlap, and their sum is an estimate, not a reading: a recorded discount may have been a real one.

- He steered customers to cash: 160 of his 381 orders (en) in the 26 weeks were paid in cash, 42 %, against 17 % for anyone else.
- 35 of those cash orders carry a discount of 15 to 30 % that nobody approved: 22 % of his cash orders, against 3 % for every other advisor in the company.
- Second and third ways: per market, below.
- 49 of his planted orders sit on the walk-in record, which holds 240 orders at full size.
- **After:** a discount above 10 % of the bill before tax needs the approval of the shop's senior, recorded on the order. Since the rule `west` has four such cash orders: two approved by slot 22, two of 12 % by slot 23 with no approval, both in the last 30 days. No other order in any shop breaks the rule.
