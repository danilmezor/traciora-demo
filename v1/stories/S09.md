## Wrinkle

The meeting is held before the shops open, so that nobody leaves a counter for it: it ends before the home shops' doors open. In `west`'s own time that is an hour and three quarters before its opening. Slot 22's answers on the regular occurrences follow a fixed cycle of eleven, in order: declined; accepted, absent; accepted, came; accepted, absent; declined; accepted, absent; accepted, came; declined; accepted, absent; declined; accepted, came. The week it was at 10:00 he accepted and came. Nobody has raised it: there is no card and no note about it.

The counts depend on T and on the market's calendar, so the seeder emits them. With 22 regular occurrences the cycle gives the first draft's numbers: 9 declined (the cancelled week among them), 15 accepted, 7 attended of 23 held.
