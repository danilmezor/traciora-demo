## Wrinkle

In the morning block (the first two and a half hours) one advisor stood at the counter checking cars in and the phones rang out: 31 % of in-hours calls missed, against 8 % in the rest of the day. A missed caller waited a median of 3 h 10 min for a call back or to get through; 18 % never did.

**The fix:** a desk shift from opening and the required cover at the desk raised from one to two for the morning block. Since then 12 % are missed in the morning and 8 % in the rest of the day; the median wait is 40 minutes; two named callers were never called back.

**Still wrong:** on the three busiest-weekday mornings since the fix seven calls were missed, 19 % of 36 at full size, against about 10 % on the other mornings.

Rule R8: a missed call has no person. A desk's rate is the misses among calls that rang that desk first. Desk 103 misses 1.7 times the shop's rate (en), 23 % over the last 90 days.

Planted lower and named, so they cannot be the answer: Saturday mornings at `main` (en, 7 %, two at the desk) and calls outside opening hours (flagged `out_of_hours`, in no desk's rate).
