## Wrinkle

- `V2` delivers on the promised day 62 % of the time, `V1` 93 %. 14 % of `west`'s orders spend more than a day waiting for parts, 4 % at the home shops.
- At T five cars wait for parts at `west`: 9, 6, 4, 2 and 1 days. The home shops have three (2 and 1 days at `main`, 1 at `east`).
- **The brakes have two causes, and the data separates them.** The cheap pads (Calder ValueStop / BREMHOF, sold only by `V2`, fitted only at `west`) come back four times as often as other pads whoever fits them. Slot 27 fits 60 % of `west`'s pad jobs, and on other pads his jobs come back at 5 % against 2 % for everyone else in the company. The cheap brand is on 45 % of pad jobs for him and for his colleagues alike, which is what lets the two effects be told apart.
