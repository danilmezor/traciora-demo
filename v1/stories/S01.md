## Wrinkle

- **A5 left.** Two pad jobs on its vans, fitted by slot 27 with the cheap brand, came back. The bill that held the second one is disputed and unpaid. Its manager rang on the Tuesday of week T-19; slot 22 promised a call back and made none. He rang three more times in nine days; nobody answered and nobody called back. No order since. Two of the company's own rules were not kept, and no record shows anyone noticing: slot 22 did not pass the complaint to slot 4 (the complaints page sends a company account's complaint to him the same day), and when slot 3's collection calls of day 10 and day 20 reached A5's accounts office and were told the bill was disputed, nobody acted on it.
- **A6 is fading**: eight visits in a 30-day block before P, then 6, 4, 3, 2 and 1. A new contact from P, the price rise on P, five estimates that waited two days or more for an answer (four then declined or expired), a quote sent two days late, a chat that waited two days.
- **A1 pays later**: visits steady at 24; its bills were paid in about 30 days and are now paid in about 50.
- A2, A3 and A4 are healthy. A2 has the most visits (26 a block, a standing Monday slot of five vans); A1 brings the most money.
- Rule R13: call 21 is hand-written with a clarification loop. A6's new contact asks for "the usual on the white van"; slot 5 asks twice (which van, what was done last) before the job is clear. Resolved; it counts for his need-discovery check.
