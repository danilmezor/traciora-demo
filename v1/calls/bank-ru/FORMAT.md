# Russian call bank: format

The pieces from which the generator assembles the Russian background calls of «Ермак-Сервис» (`../DESIGN.md` sections 2, 3 and 6). **The format is `../bank-en/FORMAT.md`**: the same file names, the same skeleton, beat, role, job-block, voice, unclear-opening and advisor-file shapes, so one generator reads both banks. This file holds only what Russian adds, and the Russian slot table. Every line is clean speech written in Russian from nothing; the recognition model of section 4 is applied by the generator afterwards. The 33 story calls in `../ru/` set the register; nothing here names a story's customer, dispute or date.

Check: `python3 ../../checks/check_bank_ru.py` (output `../../checks/bank_ru_check.txt`, sample `assembled-sample.md`). For the owner: `_README.md`.

## Files

As in the English bank, plus `forms.json`: the closed lists of Russian forms (78 cars in six cases, people, days, hours, years, fleet units, parts, lost items) and the number rules. `job-blocks.json`, the three `skeletons-*.json`, `voices.json`, `unclear.json`, `advisors/<slot>.json` carry the keys of the English files and the extra keys listed under "Russian additions".

## Russian additions

1. **A slot states the form it needs: `{slot.form}`.** `{car_model}` is the nominative, `{car_model.acc}` the accusative («на Гранту»). The forms a slot may be asked for are in the table below; the check fails on any other. Where a form is needed the value comes from a **closed list in `forms.json`** (a car, a person, a day, an hour, a year, a part) or from a **rule the generator applies without a morphology library** (money, phones, plates, order numbers: `forms.json` `money.rule` and the rules below). Wherever a line could be worded so that an open value stays in the nominative, it is: a customer's name is only ever an address («Сергей, смотрите»), a company's and a supplier's name only an apposition («компания {company_name}»), a part is named after a typed «запчасть» or «деталь» that carries the agreement.
2. **Gender markers.** `{a:записал|записала}` takes the first form for a male advisor and the second for a female one; `{c:сам|сама}` does the same by the caller's sex. Advisor files are written in the person's own gender and need none; job blocks, voices, unclear openings and the tiered lines of skeletons use them. A voice may fix the caller's sex (`"caller_sex": "m"` on `driver`).
3. **Slots that appear only when the advisor knows the thing.** `{name_comma}` is the caller's name with a comma and a space («Сергей, »), `{comma_name}` a comma, a space and the name («, Сергей»), `{car_comma}` the model with a comma («Поло, »); each is empty until the name (a `give_name` beat, a `recognise` beat, a caller's line marked `"names": true`) or the car (a `give_car` beat, a known caller, any existing-job call) has been said. So an advisor can use a name the way the person does without ever addressing a stranger by it.
4. **Numbers said aloud.** No digit is typed anywhere. Money is rendered by the advisor's `price_style`: `short` («двенадцать пятьсот двадцать»), `full` («двенадцать тысяч пятьсот двадцать»), `full_rub` (the same and «рублей»), `round` (the shop manager's «семь с половиной»: to 500 ₽ above two thousand, to 100 ₽ below); a customer's sum is `short`. `.acc` changes «одна» to «одну» and «тысяча» to «тысячу» and nothing else, so a sum is never put after «от», «до», «около». The word «рублей» is never typed after a slot. A phone is said by groups 3-3-2-2, an order number by pairs, a plate as the feminine ordinal of its digits («восемьсот шестьдесят седьмая»), a year as an ordinal («четырнадцатый», «четырнадцатого года»), a clock time by `forms.json` `hours` («в десять утра», «на десять», «к десяти», «десять ноль-ноль»).
5. **Clauses and sentences.** A job's `customer_says`, `questions`, answers, `since_answers`, `explain`, `price_statement`, `inspection_line` and `found` are lower-case clauses with no final stop; `explain` begins with «это» so that it follows «Скорее всего,», «Похоже,» or stands alone. `depends` and `answer_0/1/2` are sentences, capitalised and stopped, and are placed only after a sentence end. The generator capitalises after `.`, `?`, `!` and at the start of a line, never after `...`, and puts a full stop on a line that lacks one.
6. **A job's name has four forms**, lists of equal length read by one index: `job_said` (nominative: «замена масла»), `job_said_na` (`{job_said.na}`: «на замену масла»), `job_inf` (infinitive, the form that fits «надо», «можно», «предлагаем»: «поменять масло»), `job_do` (what the shop does at the visit: «меняем масло и масляный фильтр»). `found` is what a technician sees («в шаровой опоре люфт»).
7. **Levels.** `asked_for_booking["1"]` is positional: wordings 1 and 2 offer a booking the caller declines (voice `decline`; outcome `customer_will_call`), wordings 3 and 4 promise a call with no date (voice `accept_callback`, or `decline`; outcome `callback_promised`). After an accepted promise the recap is `moves.recap_step` when the dealt recap level is 1 or 2. `price_with_next_step["2"]` ends in `{next_step}`, the advisor's own move to a booking from `moves.next_step`; it is empty when the caller has already named a time (the rubric counts a time already agreed as the step). `objection` at levels 0 and 1 holds the advisor's own words, written for the commonest doubt, «дорого»: they are said to a doubt about price (types `price`, `compare`), and at level 0 to any doubt that was not a question; to a doubt about time or trust, and to «я подумаю», the generator says the objection's own `answer_0` / `answer_1`. `moves.closing_booked` («ждём вас») is said only when a visit is agreed, `moves.closing_callback` («наберу вас») after a call the advisor promised, `moves.closing_open` in every other case. In `price_with_next_step["2"]` the inspection wording carries no `{depends}`: the inspection line says itself what the price hangs on.
8. **Extra keys.** Advisor file: `gender`, `price_style`, `name_forms`, `moves.recap_step`, `moves.next_step`, `moves.closing_callback`, `moves.tatar` (the two Kazan advisors who answer a Tatar greeting in kind). Voice: `accept_callback`, `caller_sex`. Beat: `names`. Job block: `part` (the part a customer of this job waits for, nominative and accusative, singular so that «придёт» and «будет» agree; null where nothing is ordered: the part-late skeletons and the supplier calls about a part take only jobs that have one), `symptom` (false for maintenance and other work that is not a fault: the advisor's level-2 discovery question is then a `{job_question}` wording, never «давно так?»). `generic_objections.compare`: the doubt of skeleton NE12, «а там дешевле», with `{competitor_place}` and `{competitor_price}`. Supplier and outbound skeletons: `vendors`, the suppliers of `vendors.json` who can make or take that call. Existing-job skeleton: `voice_filter`. Existing-job skeleton: `recap` — `{"do": [...], "said": [...]}` replaces `{job_do}` and `{job_said}` in the advisor's recap wording (a car handed back is «выдаём машину», not the job); with the English bank's `recap_kind: "callback"` the recap is the advisor's own `moves.recap_step` (the Russian bank has no file-level `callback_recap`) and the closing `moves.closing_callback`; `agreed_time: "next_day"` when the agreed time cannot be today (the English `agreed_time` phrases are not used: `{time_chosen}` is always a drawn day and hour with its forms). Unclear opening: `opening[i]` goes with `second_answer[i]`; an opening carries no greeting (the voice's opener brings it); `ask`, when present, is the advisor's first question by tier and is said instead of `moves.ask_what` (an opening such as «Ну что, сделали?» is not answered with «а что случилось?»); an opening applies to one kind of call, and on an existing-job call `{need}` is the first line of the skeleton it is laid over, as in the English bank; when a call stays unclear the caller's `unresolved_end` comes before `asked_for_booking` at level 0 and after it at level 1. `existing_fits` limits an opening to the kinds of existing-job skeleton it fits («Ну что, сделали?» is not laid over a moved booking). An unclear opening is not laid over a company's call (NE11): a dispatcher states the company first.
9. **Kazan.** `forms.json` `west_tatar`: in a small share of `west` calls the caller greets and takes leave in Tatar and the call goes on in Russian; an advisor with `moves.tatar` answers in kind. Nothing else in the bank is in Tatar.
10. **What may be typed.** «сегодня», «утром», «вечером», «вчера», «на этой неделе», «на следующий день» are true on any call's day and may be typed. «завтра», «послезавтра», weekday and month names may not: they float (rule R6) and come from a slot. A symptom's age («недели две»), a mileage («сто двадцать тысяч километров») and a count («обе оси») are speech, not amounts. Seasons are not named: the tyre block says «по сезону», so one block serves both windows.
11. **Tiers.** `exact`: slots 5, 29, 24. `warm`: 6, 16, 7, 17. `curt`: 22, 23. Tiered lines carry gender markers in every tier. Skeleton EJ24 (the caller asks for the manager) is not dealt to slot 22, who is the manager.
12. **Fillers.** A voice's `filler` is put only before a line that does not begin with an answer («да», «нет», «ну», «ой», «хорошо»…) or a slot; an advisor's `fillers` («Так,», «Смотрите,») go before a generic question or offer of theirs (the car, the circumstance, the time, the name, the number, a level-1 booking offer) in three calls of ten, never before a tiered line, a greeting, a recap or a closing. A filler is dropped when the advisor's line just before it holds the same word, and a bare one- or two-word acknowledgement is dropped when the next line of the same speaker opens with its word («Да-да. Да-да, записал» is said once).
13. **Dealing wordings.** The sample assembler deals each list from a shuffled bag: a wording is not said again before the others of its list have been, per advisor for the advisor's lists and across the whole set for skeleton, voice and unclear lists. A greeting is dealt by what is actually said once `{greet}` is filled, so «Ермак-Сервис, Алина, добрый день» is not repeated while another of her greetings for that hour is unused. A generator should do the same; a person does not open three calls in a row with the same sentence unless it is their greeting. Two assembly rules keep a dealt line true to the call: when a caller's `customer_says` wording is of kind `wrong` (the caller names a part the work is not about) and the skeleton has no `explain` beat, the advisor's `explain` move is said just before the price, or before the offered time when no price was asked; and when both offered times fall on one day, `{time_chosen.day}` in the caller's `pick_of_two` names the hour («в семь»), not the day.
14. **Not used from the English format**: the advisor keys `greetings`, `greeting_tails` and `address` (a Russian advisor's four level-2 greetings are the greetings; address by name is `{name_comma}` and `{comma_name}`, written into the wordings where that person uses a name), the `{"parts": [...]}` form of a voice role, `callback_recap`. Of the slot table: `{sir_maam}` (Russian has no such address; a caller says «девушка» or «молодой человек» through a marker), `{customer_email_said}`, `{price_otd}` (a Russian advisor says the sum once; VAT is on the bill), `{tow_name}`, `{po_said}`, `{boss_first}`, `{day_next}`.
