## When a statement is overdue

| Days past the due date | What I do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Send the statement again by e-mail with a polite line |
| 10 | Telephone the person who pays the bills |
| 20 | Telephone again, and send a letter. Tell Claire |
| 45 | No new work is started for the account without Claire's approval |

A dispute about one invoice does not stop the clock on the others. A disputed invoice is passed to Greg the day it is raised, with the order number.

No account has been stopped at 45 days. Nickajack Delivery would not be stopped without Claire and Ray both saying so.
