# Missionary Ridge Driving School

*Angie, Bonny Oaks · Last reviewed {{T-6mo|date}}*

Seven training cars, Corollas, Elantras, a Sentra and a Civic, and they are growing. Pays in 15 days, list prices.

- **Contact:** Rob Atchley, lead instructor, looks after the cars: (423) 555-0195. He approves up to $500. Above that it is Denise Atchley, the owner: (423) 555-0154.
- The instructors who drop the cars may approve up to $300, the same as any company driver. They usually ring Rob anyway.
- Learners are hard on brakes, clutches (none of theirs are manual, thank goodness), tires and curbs. Brakes are measured at every oil service and alignment is checked whenever a tire shows edge wear.
- **The instructor's brake pedal** on the passenger side is fitted by an outside company. We check that it moves freely and returns, and we write it on the inspection. We do not adjust or repair it. If it is not right, the car does not go back out: call Rob.
- A car can never be off the road on a Saturday: that is their busiest day. Book their work Monday to Thursday.
