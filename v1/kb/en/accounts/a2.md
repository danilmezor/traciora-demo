# Millsaps Heating & Plumbing

*Angie, Bonny Oaks · Last reviewed {{T-4mo|date}}*

Twenty-five vehicles, mostly service vans, a few trucks. They have been with us longer than I have, and they come every single week.

## The Monday slot

Every Monday at 7:30 we take **five Millsaps vans**. Tina Ragsdale in their dispatch sends me the unit numbers and what each one needs by Friday noon. On Monday mornings their vans go first, ahead of everything else at Bonny Oaks. If Monday is a holiday the slot moves to Tuesday.

Their technicians are on call, so a van that comes in Monday is back with them by 3:30 unless I have called Tina by noon to say otherwise.

## Approvals

- The drivers drop the vans and leave. A driver may approve up to $300 like any account's driver, but they would rather not: call Tina.
- Tina Ragsdale approves up to $1,000 a vehicle: (423) 555-0121.
- Over $1,000, Hal Millsaps himself: (423) 555-0163. He answers.

## Billing

Company rate. Joyce Millsaps pays the statement and wants it on paper, mailed, with the invoices behind it. Donna knows.

## Things I know about these vans

- Units are M-101 to M-125 and the number is on the rear door.
- The Express vans eat front brakes. Measure the pads at every visit and tell Tina a visit ahead, so it is never a surprise.
- The ladder racks sit lower than our door at bay 2. Bay 1 or bay 4.
