## The purchase-order number

**Every order needs Nickajack's purchase-order number before the van is worked on.** Lonnie gives it when he books. If a driver turns up without one, call Lonnie and get it before you open the order. It goes in the purchase-order field, not in the notes. An invoice without it is sent back by their accounts payable and is not paid until it is fixed.

## The fleet

Ford Transit 250, Ram ProMaster 2500, Ford Transit Connect and two Mercedes Sprinters (units ND-34 and ND-35). Unit numbers are ND-01 to ND-35 and are on the driver's door.

- **Fleet PM service A** every 5,000 miles: [what it includes](../services/maintenance/fleet-pm-service.md). Lonnie sends the units that are due each week.
- The Sprinters are diesel. We do their fluids, filters, brakes and tires. Engine and emissions work on them goes to the dealer; tell Lonnie, do not attempt it.
- Vans are dropped by 7:30 and needed back by 3:00 for the afternoon routes. If one will not make 3:00, Lonnie hears by noon.

## Approvals

A driver may approve up to $300 for the visit, as for every account. Above that, Lonnie Coffey. Terms, prices and the rest: [Company accounts](company-account-terms.md).

## The quarterly review

Every quarter Greg and Claire sit down with Lonnie Coffey and Stuart Haney. We bring: visits and spend by month, vans due for brakes and tires next quarter, comebacks, and how long statements are taking to be paid.
