# Opening a company account

*Claire Brennan · Last reviewed {{T-10mo|date}}*

1. **Five vehicles or more**, and at least six months as a customer paying at the counter.
2. The business fills in the credit application (Donna has the form) and names one person who approves work and one who pays bills. They may be the same person.
3. I approve it and set the credit limit. Nobody else opens an account.
4. Terms: **15 days** for a fleet of fewer than ten vehicles, **30 days** from ten up.
5. Greg writes the account's page in this folder and adds the row to the [terms table](company-account-terms.md): approver, anything the shop needs to know. No page, no account.
6. Donna sets the customer up in the shop system with its terms. From that day its vehicles are not charged at the counter.

The company rate is not part of opening an account. Two customers have it, by agreement with me, and that is not a thing to offer.
