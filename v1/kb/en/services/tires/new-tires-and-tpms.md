## New tires

On the order: **Mount & balance 4 new tires**, plus the tires. Mounting and balancing four is $100.00. On the common sedan size a set fitted comes to:

| Tier | Tire | Each | Set of four, fitted |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Economy | Milestar | $100.00 | $519.40 |
| Standard | Hankook Kinergy | $143.59 | $693.76 |
| Premium | Michelin Defender | $220.51 | $1,001.44 |

The fitted price includes $19.40 of tire fees for four. Every other size is a call to Marcus for price and arrival.

- Read the size off the sidewall, not off the door sticker; cars get re-shod.
- We sell tires in pairs or fours, not singles, unless the other three are nearly new.
- Offer the [alignment](alignment.md). Say why.
- Tires arrive next morning in Chattanooga. **In Nashville they come Tuesdays and Fridays.**
- We do not fit tires the customer brings if they are more than six years old by the date code.

## Tire-pressure sensors

**Replace TPMS sensor & relearn (one wheel)**: half an hour, $128.86. The batteries inside die at about ten years, one wheel after another. When one goes on a ten-year-old car, tell the customer the others are the same age. Cheapest to do with new tires, when the tire is off anyway.

## Seasonal changeover

A few customers who drive to the mountains keep winter tires. Changeover is on the price list. Storage we do for a handful of sets; ask Marcus before offering it.
