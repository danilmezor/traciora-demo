# Mounts and exhaust

*Travis Burnette · Last reviewed {{@price_rise+3wd:main|date}}*

**Replace engine mount (one)**: 1.5 hours, $307.05. A thump when shifting from park to drive, a shudder at idle with the A/C on, an engine that rocks when you blip the throttle. Found on the lift with a pry bar and a photo of the torn rubber. Engines have three or four mounts; we replace the ones that have failed, and say so if another is on its way.

**Replace muffler / rear exhaust section**: 1.2 hours, $384.73. Loud, or hanging. A rusted-through muffler is a simple job. Anything forward of it is not on the list.

**Catalytic converters** are not on the price list. They vary from a few hundred dollars to several thousand by car, they are a theft target, and a converter that has failed was usually killed by something else, a misfire or an engine burning oil, that has to be fixed first. So it is always a [diagnosis](../diagnostics/check-engine-light.md) and then a one-off estimate from Travis.

We do not weld. A pipe that needs fabricating goes to an exhaust shop, and we tell the customer which.
