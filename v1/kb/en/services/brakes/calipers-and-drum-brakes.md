# Calipers and drum brakes

*Angie Holloway · Last reviewed {{@price_rise+3wd:main|date}}*

**Replace front brake caliper (one side), bleed**: 1.3 hours, $316.41 with a remanufactured caliper. Priced per side. A caliper is replaced when it leaks or when the piston or slides have seized: the signs are one pad worn to nothing while the other looks new, a wheel that is hot after a drive, or the car pulling under braking. It is usually found during a pad job, which is why the customer hears about it by phone and not at the start. Have the photo.

We replace the one that failed. If the other side is the same age and the boot is torn, we say so and let the customer choose.

**Replace rear brake shoes & hardware (drum)**: 2 hours, $351.81. Smaller and older cars and some trucks still have drums at the back. Shoes last a long time, often 100,000 miles, so the customer has usually never heard of them. "It is the same job as pads, on the older kind of brake. More fiddly, which is why it is two hours."

A caliper carries a core charge to us until the old one goes back. The customer does not see it: [Returns and cores](../../parts/returns-and-cores.md).
