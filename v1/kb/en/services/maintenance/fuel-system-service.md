# Fuel system service

*Travis Burnette · Last reviewed {{@price_rise+3wd:main|date}}*

Two jobs.

**Fuel induction & injector cleaning service**: 0.8 h, $151.18. Cleaner run through the intake and the injectors. It helps direct-injection engines past 60,000 miles that idle rough or hesitate, because their intake valves coke up. It does nothing for a car that runs fine, and we do not sell it to one.

**Replace fuel filter**: 0.7 h, $121.77. Only on vehicles that have a serviceable one, which today means older trucks and some imports. Most cars built since about 2010 have the filter inside the tank for life. If the catalogue does not offer it for the car, the car does not have one.

Customers ask for "injector cleaner" because a bottle at the parts store promised a lot. Be straight: if there is a drivability complaint, the first job is a [diagnosis](../diagnostics/check-engine-light.md), not a cleaning.
