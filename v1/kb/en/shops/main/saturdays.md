# East Main: Saturdays

*Greg*

Saturday 8:00 to 1:00, East Main only. Two at the desk: Brenda every Saturday, and one of the three weekday advisors in turn. Two technicians, in turn. The turn is on the schedule; to give up your Saturday, ask for a change of date in the app and name who takes the shift.

**We take:** oil services, tires, rotations, batteries, wipers and bulbs, brake jobs that were inspected during the week and have their parts on the shelf, and pick-ups of cars finished on Friday.

**We do not take:** diagnostics (Kevin does not work Saturdays), anything that needs a part ordered that morning (one parts run, at 9:00, and it is not guaranteed), anything over three hours.

- The last car in is 11:30.
- If a Saturday customer needs a diagnosis, book Monday with Kevin's column and say so plainly. Do not let a technician "have a quick look" that turns into a car stuck on a lift over the weekend.
- Company vehicles are welcome on Saturday for maintenance. Driver limits apply as on any day.
- Bonny Oaks' phone is forwarded to East Main on Saturday mornings. Take the message and book into Bonny Oaks' Monday.
