# East Main: how the day runs

*Travis · Last reviewed {{@early_desk|date}}*

**7:30 to 10:00 is the whole game.** Four cars in ten arrive in the first hour and a half, and that is also when the phone rings most. It used to be one advisor checking cars in while the phone rang out. Since {{@early_desk|date}} there are two of us at the desk for those hours: one takes the line of people, the other takes the phone and does nothing else. Do not both go out to the lot.

- Mondays are the worst morning. If you are the phone person on a Monday, you do not leave the desk until 10:00, for anything.
- Randy hands out the work at 7:30 from the day's appointments. A car that was not booked goes to him before you promise it to anyone.
- **Diagnostics go to Kevin** and he takes about four a day. Book check-engine and electrical jobs into his column, not "any tech". When his column is full it is full: offer the next day, or Bonny Oaks, where Andre takes overflow.
- Lunch is staggered between 11:30 and 1:30. The desk is never empty and the phone is never on voicemail in opening hours.
- Parts runs land about 9:15, 12:15 and 3:15. Call customers about "is my car ready" after a run, not before.
- Ready calls go out by 4:30. After 5:00 people are driving and do not answer.
- Thursday mornings Ray is in the waiting room with a coffee, talking to whoever is there. Customers like it. If he quotes anybody a price, it does not count; he knows.
