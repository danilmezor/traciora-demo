# Charlotte Pike: opening and closing

*Dale · changed by Claire Brennan {{@discount_rule|date}}*

Open at 7:30. Whoever is first unlocks and empties the key box.

Float is $150.

Close at 5:30. Cars locked, keys in the cabinet.

**The till is counted by two people, every day, and both initial the count sheet.** The advisor who closes counts; Dale counts again. If Dale is out, the second count is the other advisor's. A difference of more than $5 either way is written on the sheet with the reason and reported to the office at East Main the same evening.

**The deposit goes to the bank every working day**, by Dale.
