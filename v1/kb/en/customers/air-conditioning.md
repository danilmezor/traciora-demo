# Air conditioning not cold

From May to August this is what a lot of our phone calls are about.

## Start with a check

An A/C performance check is $49.95. We measure vent temperature and system pressures and look for leaks with a UV light. It tells us whether the system is just low or something has failed.

## Recharge

Customers call it "freon" or "a recharge". On the bill it is *evacuate and recharge*: we recover what is in the system, pull a vacuum to check it holds, and refill by weight.

| Your car uses | Typical cars | Price |
|---|---|---|
| R-134a | Most cars up to about 2016 | $207.40 |
| R-1234yf | Most cars from about 2017 on | $278.80 |

The newer refrigerant costs several times more per pound, which is the whole difference. The label under the hood says which one your car takes.

A system that was empty has a leak. A recharge without finding the leak lasts weeks, not years, and we will tell you so before you pay for it.

## Musty smell

A sour smell when the A/C starts is growth on the evaporator. A cleaning service is $84.95, and a fresh [cabin air filter](maintenance-by-mileage.md) usually goes with it.
