# Tires, flats and alignment

## Flat or slow leak

A nail in the tread can usually be repaired: patch-plug from the inside and rebalance, $31.00. A hole in the sidewall or shoulder cannot be repaired safely, and we will show you why.

## New tires

We fit the tires we sell. Four tires for a typical sedan, mounted and balanced, run from about $519.40 to $1,001.44 depending on the brand. That includes the disposal and state tire fees. Most sizes arrive the next morning.

## Rotation and balance

Rotation is $25.00 and should happen with every other oil change. Balancing four wheels is $60.00; ask for it if the steering wheel shakes at highway speed.

## Alignment

A four-wheel alignment is $119.95. Get one with new tires, after a hard pothole hit, or when the car drifts or the steering wheel sits off-centre.

## Tire pressure light

If the light stays on after you set the pressures, a sensor in one wheel has probably died. Replacing one sensor is about $128.86.
