# Hybrids and electric cars

**Hybrids:** we do the ordinary service: oil, filters, brakes, tires, alignment, suspension, the 12-volt battery, coolant. Hybrid work is done at East Main only. We do not repair the high-voltage battery, the inverter or the drive motor; for those we will point you to a dealer.

**Electric cars:** tires, alignment and brakes. Nothing else.
