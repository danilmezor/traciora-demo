Tell us. We would rather hear it from you than read it somewhere.

1. Call the shop that did the work and ask for the service manager: Greg Whitaker for East Main and Bonny Oaks, Dale Hutchins for Charlotte Pike. Or tell any advisor and they will get him.

3. If the car needs to come back, we look at it first, ahead of the day's other work, and it does not cost you anything to have it looked at.

If you are still not satisfied, write to Claire Brennan, managing owner: cbrennan@brennanautocare.example.
