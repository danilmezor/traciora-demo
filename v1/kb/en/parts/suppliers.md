## Notes

- Sequoyah Parts Supply: first call for anything. On the truck they said, nine times in ten. When they are out of a part they say so.
- Ridge & Valley Import Parts: dearer. Use for sensors, coils, timing kits, anything where the cheap one comes back. Aisin and Denso come from here.
- Hiwassee Battery & Electric: one run a day. Miss 10:30 and it is tomorrow.
- Tellico Tire Distributors: tires for Nashville come twice a week. Do not tell a Nashville customer "tomorrow" on a Wednesday.
