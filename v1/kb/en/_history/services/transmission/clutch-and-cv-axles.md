# Clutch and CV axles

*Travis Burnette · Last reviewed {{T-9mo|date}}*

**Replace clutch kit, resurface flywheel**: 6.2 hours, $1,165.91. We see about two a month; not many people drive a stick now. The pedal bites at the very top, the revs rise without the car pulling, or it will not go into gear. The transmission comes out, so it is a full day and usually an overnight. Kit is the disc, pressure plate and release bearing; the flywheel is resurfaced. A dual-mass flywheel that cannot be resurfaced is extra, and the customer hears that possibility before we start.

**Replace CV axle shaft (one side)**: 1.6 hours, $363.64. A click-click-click on full lock in a parking lot, or grease thrown round the inside of the wheel from a split boot. We replace the whole shaft with a new one; rebooting an axle that has already run dry is false economy. Priced per side.

Neither of these is urgent in the way brakes are, and both get worse in a way the customer can hear. Tell them what the next stage sounds like.
