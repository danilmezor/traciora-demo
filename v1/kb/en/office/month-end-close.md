# Month-end close

*Donna Ledford · Last reviewed {{T-5mo|date}}*

I close the month in the first three working days of the next one. The steps are in this order for a reason.

1. **Open orders.** Every order still open from before the last week of the month is either finished and invoiced, or has a reason. I ask each shop manager for the reasons. An order open more than 60 days is reported to Claire.
2. **Till counts.** For each shop: every day has a count, and the differences for the month are added up by shop and by the person who closed. A few dollars either way is ordinary. I look for the same name short again and again.
3. **Voids.** The list of every voided order, with who voided it and whether a payment had been taken. Since {{@discount_rule|date}}, a paid order voided by anyone but a shop manager is reported the same day.
4. **Discounts.** Every bill with more than 10 % off has an approver. Claire receives the same list each Friday.
5. **Cash orders on the Walk-in customer.** There should be none.
6. **Supplier statements** against what we received: credits for returns and cores are there, or I ask Marcus.
7. **Company-account statements** go out on the first working day: [Statements and receivables](statements-and-receivables.md).
8. **Payroll hours** for the last period: [Payroll hours](payroll-hours.md).
9. **Sales tax** return, filed and paid by the 20th.

Steps 2 to 5 were added to this list, or made stricter, in {{@discount_rule|month}}. Before that I looked at the till totals by shop only. Looking at them by person is what showed the problem at Charlotte Pike.
