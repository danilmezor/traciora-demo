# How prices change

*Claire Brennan · {{@belt_proposal|date}}*

I set the labour rate and the menu prices. Marcus sets parts prices from cost, by the matrix. Nobody else changes a price.

When a price changes:

1. The [price list](price-list.md) and the [rates page](labour-rates-and-fees.md) change that morning, before we open.
2. The public [prices page](../customers/prices-for-common-services.md) changes the same morning.
3. Every service page that shows a dollar figure is checked within five working days by whoever keeps it. Travis keeps the East Main service pages, Angie the brake and suspension pages.
4. A written estimate we already gave is honoured at its price for its 30 days.

Step 3 is the one we missed. After the rise on {{@price_rise|date}} the timing belt page kept the old labour rate, an advisor quoted from it, and it cost us a discount and an unhappy customer. If you find another page like it, do not work around it: propose the fix the same day. The correction to the timing belt page has been proposed and is Greg's to decide.
