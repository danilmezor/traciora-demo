## Limits of v1

- A build more than a year from the nominal T falls back to an earlier instant on 36 English and 17 Russian days, at most seven days back: the cards' week-bound days carry no marker per day.
- Outside Gate 1's 22 days a build can read a statement red (seen: Russian 2027-02-08, two statements).
- A build from Monday to Thursday noon leaves out the trail steps and cards dated after T (`G-open-shift`; Russian also `G-weekly-callers`).
- The Russian board shows the product's English column names: the product reads only its own column file.
- East's wait from check-in to work in its first half year is recorded per build as an outcome, not held to a figure. Worst month at the nominal T: 0.46 days in English, 3.11 in Russian (a tyre-change window).
- In English the 2025 air-conditioning season starts in June, the seasonal hire's own month; "the season starts a month before the hire" shows in 2026 only.
- The senior operator's English brief has two points: the roster's one cover band shows no day short at east, while the ledger (`s5.week_off`) and card `G-cover-week-off` say one short. The Russian brief shows it.
- Before the price rise the generator rounds a drifted fluid price to 10 roubles; no statement reads prices before the rise.
- Story cards and pages quote design targets of the rows the build measures (the ledger rows marked `outcome`); the evals read the build's value from `manifest.<cfg>.json`.
- The generator keeps passes that fitted figures a band now covers (`waits`, `leave_hours`, `sold_value`).
- S05's claim that six days of leave cannot carry the lost hours has no rule a build can check.
- The Russian knowledge base says the drivers who hold a power of attorney are listed on the customer record; no file names them.
- Knowledge ranks, revisions and binaries; the Inbox lines other stores push live; `S16-review`'s attachment.
- `checks/check_calls.py --product` compares the stored scorecard version with the product's current one and reads 0 of 31; levels, scores and results agree.
