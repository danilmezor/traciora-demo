## 1. Staffing
- Respondent shops, employees: 1-2 14%, 3-4 24%, 5-6 16%, 7-8 18%, 9-10 13%, 11+ 15%. Bays: 1-2 13%, 3-4 25%, 5-6 20%, 7-8 20%, 9+ 19%. Revenue $1M-$2.49M is the most common band (30%); >$2.5M 10%. (R+W26, READ)
- Respondent roles: owner/partner 55%, manager 33%, foreman/supervisor 12%. 47% of respondents were technicians before, 31% service writers. (R+W26, READ)
- Tech pay: flat rate 25%, hourly 16%, salary 15%, hourly+commission 23%, salary+commission 15%, other 6%. (R+W26, READ)
- Avg employee tenure: <1 yr 4%, 1-2 yrs 23%, 3-4 yrs 34%, 5+ yrs 39%. (R+W26, READ)
- Work organisation: one tech per vehicle start-to-finish 39%, teams 36%, segmented by job type 23%. 53% have apprenticeship programs. (R+W26, READ)
- Ratio per bay: PartsTech "0.7 technicians per bay, 1 advisor per 3 bays" (UNVERIFIED, snippet only; origin page partstech.com/?p=6028 did not show it when opened). Consultant benchmark about 1 advisor per 1.5 techs in general repair; older 2:1 (UNVERIFIED, snippets from ratchetandwrench.com/articles/7321, motor.com BusinessSense).
- Annual tech turnover: no independent general-repair figure found. Collision proxy 30-40% (I-CAR/SCRS press release, UNVERIFIED snippet). Dealer 27.2% for 2016 (NADA, UNVERIFIED).
- BLS 2025: median pay $50,620/yr ($24.34/h); 825,800 jobs; 32% of techs work at dealers, 15% self-employed; "evening or weekend work may be required"; 5% growth 2025-35. https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/automotive-service-technicians-and-mechanics.htm (READ, page modified 2026-09-14). R+W26 quotes BLS: ~26% of techs in independent repair/maintenance vs 32% dealers.
- 29% of owners name "shortage of qualified technicians" the single biggest industry challenge; 19% rising cost of doing business. (R+W26, READ)
- Roles porter/parts person/bookkeeper: no staffing numbers found. R+W26 lists "porter" as prior position for 13% of respondents only.

- Tekmetric Shop Index (July 2026): national avg car count 118/month, ARO $704.25, parts margin 58%, ELR $134.92 (median 97 cars, $649.88, 56%, $134.31). UNVERIFIED: appears only in a search summary; the Tire Review, MOTOR and R+W pages I opened (tirereview.com/tekmetric-shop-index-shop-performance-benchmarks/, motor.com/2026/07/tekmetric-launches-free-shop-benchmarking-tool/) state no values. ## 3. Service mix and seasonality
- Share of sales from maintenance (R+W26, READ): <30% 16%, 30-39% 29%, 40-49% 14%, 50-59% 16%, 60-69% 11%, 70%+ 6%. From repair: 30-39% 24%, 40-49% 18%, 50-59% 13%, 60-69% 12%, 70%+ 15%. From tires: <5% 37%, 5-9% 22%, 10-14% 17%, 15-19% 12%, 20%+ 12%. 59% of shops sell tires; 38% do ADAS; 30% EV; 29% collision; 25% quick lane.
- Modern Tire Dealer survey (tire dealers, not general repair; UNVERIFIED snippet): auto service 48% of sales (2020); offered by dealers: brakes 92%, chassis/suspension 82%, diagnostics 65%, AC 59%; margins AC 62%, brakes and engine/diagnostics 59%. https://www.moderntiredealer.com/articles/32593-from-staffing-to-margins-survey-provides-valuable-data
- Share of RO by job type: NOT FOUND. Car Care Council/IMR ranking of common repairs (oil change first, then wiper blades, air filter, scheduled maintenance, tires, battery, brakes) is a ranking only (UNVERIFIED snippet, autoremarketing.com).
- Vehicle mileage seen in shops: ~80% have 50k-150k miles, 2% <50k, 5% >150k (OEC/PartsTech survey of ~700 shops, Sept 2025) https://partstech.com/resource/blog/how-has-customer-behavior-changed-in-the-last-year/ (READ, Apr 2026).
- Seasonality: qualitative only. Womply 2018 via R+W: Fridays busiest; May-Aug spikes; Friday before Memorial Day $1,840 avg, +41% vs typical day; revenue otherwise "fairly consistent". https://www.ratchetandwrench.com/articles/8274-taking-advantage-of-peak-days (READ, 2018 data). Spring (Mar-May) and fall busy, AC pre-summer, batteries on cold snaps, tire changeover surges (UNVERIFIED snippets, Automotive Service World, NOLN). No monthly percentages found.

## 4. Customer behaviour
- Declined/delayed work: 50% of ~700 shops say customers are delaying or declining; 49% say more cautious on non-required work vs 2024; 54% say fewer come in for proactive maintenance. https://www.motor.com/2026/04/auto-repair-cost-uncertainty/ (READ, Apr 2026, OEC survey Sept 2025). Implied decline of recommended work ~40-50% from closing ratio above (my inference).
- No-show: 10-15% of booked appointments never happen; 47% of independent shops use online scheduling; 69% use electronic customer communication. AutoLeap-sponsored R+W article (READ, vendor): link above in section 2.
- Repeat/retention: R+W 2017 article: assumed attrition 20-25%, measured 50-60% at many shops; >50% of new customers never return (UNVERIFIED snippet, ratchetandwrench.com/articles/4757). Visits per year: not found.
- Appointment vs walk-in share: NOT FOUND.
- Comeback rate: no auto-repair benchmark found. Generic targets <3% (fleet vendor), collision DRP 10.1% of ROs (CCC 2020) (both UNVERIFIED snippets).
- Warranty: 24 months/24,000 miles nationwide is the common independent-network standard (TechNet Professional, NAPA AutoCare, WorldPac Stress-Free), extendable to 36/36 with NAPA (UNVERIFIED snippets of shop pages and https://technetprofessional.com/documents/nw_terms_en.pdf).
- Estimate approval channel (text/phone): not found as a figure.

## 5. Phones and messages
- Missed calls: Marchex says up to 21% of automotive service calls missed/failed, healthy at or below 10%, best locations ~10%, appointment rate per location ranges from single digits to over 60%. https://www.marchex.com/up-to-21-percent-of-automotive-service-customer-calls-go-unanswered-creating-significant-revenue-leakage/ (READ, undated, footer 2026).
- In Motion Brands via Auto Service World (2026-02-24, READ): many shops miss 20-40% of inbound calls in busy hours; ~30% of calls arrive 7-10 a.m.; Monday most calls, Saturday fewest; call reasons: price 30%, booking 30%, general questions 26%, service issues 6%, other 8%. https://www.autoserviceworld.com/how-ai-is-helping-shops-deal-with-call-volume/ Vendor-promoting AI article.
- Calls per day: "30-50 per shop" appears only on vendor blogs (UNVERIFIED).
- CallRevu mid-2026 (dealership calls, READ): hangup rate ~2.5%; 22.4% of promised callbacks not completed; hold cap 90 s service. https://www.ratchetandwrench.com/news/researchreports/article/55396841/callrevu-releases-mid-year-2026-benchmark-report-3-takeaways-for-auto-shop-owners-can-take-to-reduce-hangups-and-increase-revenue
- CallSource (dealers, 2020, READ): 1 in 20 business-hours calls unanswered; aftermarket/quick-lube shops often open until 8-9 p.m. https://www.cbtnews.com/are-service-department-hours-in-line-with-customer-needs/

## 7. Owner worries (what the data shows)
Sourced: technician shortage (29% biggest challenge, R+W26; 59% of owners say it significantly affects operations, Marchex page); customers declining work (50%); EV/ADAS readiness (barrier: training 18%, low local EV volume 23%); 31% do not track CSI; 54% give diagnostics away free. Parts: 47% buy from 5+ vendors monthly (R+W26). Backorders, fleet late pay, cash discrepancies: no sources found. How each shows in data (my design suggestions, not sourced): estimates with status "sent, not approved" aging; ROs "complete, not picked up"; comeback = new RO same VIN and system within warranty window; parts ETA past promise date; per-shop ARO/car count/ELR gaps versus peers; deposits vs payments mismatch; fleet invoices aging buckets.
