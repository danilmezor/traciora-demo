# Russian independent car-repair chain (СТО): operating facts for a demo dataset
Research date 2026-11-13. "READ" = figure seen on the fetched page. "UNVERIFIED" = not on a page I opened (search-snippet only or general knowledge).
Source dates are given per line. Note: the fetch tool summarises pages, so figures are as extracted by it.

## 1. Staffing
- Master-receiver (мастер-приёмщик) combines workshop master (мастер цеха) and consultant (консультант) roles; pure приёмщик/консультант is a separate role. Technicians: слесарь, механик, диагност, электрик, маляр. READ. https://teletype.in/@vagminsk/pBbJSN5voaa (2021-07-12, trade consultant article)
- Load norms, same page: technician load 0.8-0.85 of ФРВ (фонд рабочего времени), about 140-150 billed нормо-часов per month; productive staff share about 60% (+-10%); administrative share not above 40-50% of headcount; ФОТ 20-25% of total revenue (labour + parts); мастер-приёмщик (combined) up to 10 заказов/day on 5/2, 8 h; приёмщик/консультант only, up to 15 average заказов/day.
- Real 4-post station (2021): 3 consultants + 6 mechanics = 9 people, outsourced accountant, owner on payroll; 2 mechanics left, 2 вахта mechanics hired from other cities; corporate clients 10-15% of clients. READ. https://t-j.ru/sto-covid/ (2021-12-29)
- Small universal 2-post shop: 2-4 mechanics + мастер-приёмщик; 26 working days/month assumed. READ. https://www.moysklad.ru/poleznoe/shkola-torgovli/kak-otkryt-avtoservis/ (2026-08-18)
- Derived (mine, not sourced): 8 posts is roughly 8-10 mechanics/diagnosticians/electricians, 2 мастера-приёмщика per shift, 1 менеджер по запчастям/кладовщик, 1 администратор, outsourced or one in-house бухгалтер, 1 руководитель СТО. UNVERIFIED as a headcount table; no source found with a ready staffing table for 6-10 posts.
- Pay: mechanics are paid сдельно, 30-40% of the work value they bring in (Kazan service owners). READ. https://www.business-gazeta.ru/article/532346 (2021-12, old). Schemes named by a shop-software vendor: % of closed заказ-наряд revenue, оплата по нормо-часам, оплата по себестоимости работ, bonus for expanding the order. READ. https://habr.com/ru/articles/716478/ (2023-02-13)
- Pay level now: Avito Работа, October 2025 offered average: автомеханик 178,900 RUB/month (+74% y/y), мастер-приёмщик about 113,000 (+22%), all auto-service roles 158,835 (+31%). READ. https://finance.mail.ru/article/avito-rabota-srednie-predlagaemye-zarplaty-v-avtoservisah-v-oktyabre-vyrosli-na-31-68693269/ (2025-11-13). Offers, not actual pay.
- Vacancy example, Yekaterinburg, автослесарь/автомеханик: 100,000-170,000 on hand, оклад + ставки нормо-часа, schedule "4/2 и другие", 12 h shift. READ. https://hh.ru/vacancy/135907630 (archived 2026-10-04)
- Wages up to 60% of a service's costs (Союз автосервисов); shortage of staff named as a main price driver. READ. https://www.infullbroker.ru/news/ceny-na-avtoservis-vyrosli-na-tret-za-god/ (2025-07-29)
- Staff turnover: no figure found. GAP.
